# After: My Shortcuts (same made up example)

Same practice, same three tasks as `before_typed_prompts.md`. All names and messages are invented.

## Setup answers

1. Weekly tasks: meeting notes, replies to patients, posts for the practice.
2. Two texts she wrote herself:
   > Hi Ben, quick one. The towel order stays the same this week. Two boxes, not more. If you are late again, tell me before 8. See you then, Mara

   > Team, short update. New booking system starts Monday. The paper calendar stays at the desk until Friday, just in case. Questions to me, not to the IT guy. Thanks, Mara
3. Readers: patients and the team.
4. Never: "journey", "don't hesitate", "exciting", exclamation marks.
5. App: Claude.

## Her Shelf and Voice Card

```
recap | you paste: messy notes from a meeting or call | you get: 5 short bullet points, then 1 open question | voice: no
reply | you paste: a message you got, plus what you want to say in a few words | you get: a ready reply, 80 words or less | voice: yes
post | you paste: one idea or one thing that happened | you get: a social post in 3 short paragraphs, no hashtags, no emojis | voice: yes
```

```
LENGTH: short sentences, mostly 4 to 10 words; messages 4 to 6 lines
TONE: opens with the first name and "quick one" or "short update"; plan first, reason after; clear limits like "two boxes, not more"; signs off with "See you then" or "Thanks" and her first name
I SAY: quick one; short update; stays the same; not more; see you then
I NEVER SAY: journey; don't hesitate; exciting; exclamation marks
SAMPLE: Team, short update. New booking system starts Monday. The paper calendar stays at the desk until Friday, just in case. Questions to me, not to the IT guy. Thanks, Mara
```

## Task 1

**She types:** `recap:` and pastes the same notes.

**Back (voice: no):**

- New booking system planned from the 1st of next month [check: date confirmed?]
- Team needs a 1 hour training first (Jonas)
- Laundry service was late twice; call them
- Lea is away 2 weeks in December; no cover yet
- Reception phone is busy at 8; second line is an idea, budget unknown

Open question: Who covers Lea's 2 weeks in December?

## Task 2

**She types:** `reply:` and pastes the same message and the same few words.

**Back (voice: yes, 36 words):**

> Hi Tom, quick one. Keep Thursday at 4. It's the last check before you run again. Until then a short, easy jog is fine. If it hurts, stop and tell me Thursday. See you then, Mara

## Task 3

**She types:** `post:` and pastes the same idea.

**Back (voice: yes):**

> Short update from the practice. This week one of our patients walked up the stairs to her flat. Without the rail. First time since her hip surgery.
>
> What helped her: [check: which exercises, and for how long].
>
> Stuck after surgery? Ask us at the desk. Thanks to her for letting us share this. Mara
