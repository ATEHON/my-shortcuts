# Before: the same prompts, typed every week (made up example)

All names, places and messages here are invented.
Mara runs a small physio practice called Birchwood Physio. Every week she asks her AI for the same three things.
She types the whole instruction each time, then pastes her input under it.

---

## Task 1: meeting notes

**What she types (194 characters):**

> Turn these team meeting notes into a short summary. Use 5 short bullet points and add one open question at the end that we still need to decide. No intro and no closing line. Here are the notes:

**Her input:**

> team mtg mon. new booking system from 1st of next month?? jonas says we need training, 1h. towels: laundry service late twice, call them. lea on holiday 2 wks in dec, cover? reception phone always busy at 8, maybe 2nd line. budget for 2nd line unknown.

## Task 2: reply to a patient

**What she types (195 characters):**

> Write a reply to this patient message. Keep it under 80 words, use their first name, no exclamation marks, and make it sound like me, not like a robot. Here is the message and what I want to say:

**Her input:**

> Message: "Hi, I have my appointment on Thursday at 4 but my knee feels much better. Do I still need to come? And can I start running again? Tom"
> What I want to say: keep the appointment, it is the last check before running. short easy jog is ok if no pain. see you thursday.

**What came back (no Voice Card, 78 words):**

> Hi Tom, thank you for your message, and it is great to hear that your knee is feeling much better. I would still recommend keeping your appointment on Thursday at 4, as it is our last check before you return to running. Until then, a short and easy jog is fine as long as you feel no pain. Please don't hesitate to get in touch if anything changes. Looking forward to seeing you on Thursday. Best regards, Mara

It follows the rules she typed. It still does not sound like her: long sentences, "don't hesitate", a formal sign off.

## Task 3: social post

**What she types (166 characters):**

> Write an Instagram post for our physio practice about the idea below. Three short paragraphs, no hashtags, no emojis. Sound like me, not like an ad. Here is the idea:

**Her input:**

> a patient walked up the stairs to her flat without the rail this week, first time since her hip surgery. she said we can share it.

---

**Instructions typed for the three tasks: 555 characters, every week.**
"Sound like me" is in two prompts, but the AI has nothing that shows how she sounds.
